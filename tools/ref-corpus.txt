In ancient Greek mythology, Elysion was a legendary paradise reserved for heroes, a realm of peace and eternal happiness.
Inspired by it, Elysion is a multichain token launchpad that makes token creation simpler across blockchain ecosystems.
One creation. One payment. Multiple chains.
Every launch is verifiable onchain: token name and ticker, network, contract address, transaction hashes and timestamps.
These reduce, but cannot eliminate, sniping and coordinated buying.
The contracts are not yet audited.
Every legend has an origin. Every token needs a beginning.
